# Engineering review: 2026-11-25

Four reviewers (Skeptic: logic and security, Architect: structure, Advocate: UX, Artisan: visual) went over the whole codebase after the currencies feature. Regression tests for the fixes are in `tests/Feature/Admin/ReviewFixesTest.php`.

## Fixed
- **Base-currency change:**
  - Both rows are re-read under a lock, so a double click can no longer convert prices twice.
  - Past orders' `exchange_rate` and `base_total` are re-expressed against the new base.
  - The POS catalog cache is flushed.
- **Checkout:** the posted `currency` must match the current currency and rate, otherwise the order is refused.
- **Customer accounts:** `users.*` no longer reaches customer accounts (`UserPolicy::isCustomer`).
- **Trader prices:**
  - Only customer-only accounts get them, so a trader later made staff or a sales rep pays retail.
  - Setting them needs `price_tiers.update`, not just `products.update`.
- **Company orders:** an on-account order can only be deleted once it is cancelled (`OrderPolicy::delete`), so company debt can't be erased.
- **Activity log:** the "users" channel, which includes customer changes, needs `users.view`.
- **Catalog:** the "on sale" filter and the price range respect the sale start and end dates (`CatalogQuery::saleActiveSql`).
- **Currencies:**
  - The rate is typed as "1 USD = X ₪".
  - New currencies start inactive.
  - The make-default warning shows a concrete example and says the conversion can't be undone exactly.
  - A rate floor stops prices being converted to 0.
- **Small conversions:** the minimum-order shortfall never shows as 0.00.
- **UX:**
  - The checkout scrolls to the first error, shows a loading label and states the payment currency.
  - The Thanks page keeps the phone number's direction.
  - Quantity buttons have proper labels.
  - The mobile menu shows a visible currency label.
  - Product create explains where photos go.
- **Visual:**
  - Badges are 12px by default.
  - The KPI shows «جديد» instead of ">999%".
  - Admin dark mode uses `color-scheme: dark`, and the primary colour keeps white text at AA contrast.
  - The cart grid no longer stretches.
  - The avatar ring uses the palette colours.
  - The loader is calmer in admin: a 2px bar and a lighter dim.
- **Ops:**
  - Nightly `backup:run` and `backup:clean`, plus a weekly `queue:prune-failed`.
  - CLAUDE.md deploy steps now say `route:trans:cache`.

## Open (decisions or larger work)
- **Before launch:**
  - An offsite backup disk (S3 or B2), with one restore tested.
  - Error tracking and daily logs.
  - Redis for cache, queue and sessions.
  - `SETTINGS_CACHE_ENABLED`.
- **Order history:** use soft deletes for orders and company payments, and reverse a payment with a counter-entry instead of deleting it.
- **Missing store features:** coupons (`discount_total` is always 0), refunds and payment status, invoices or PDF receipts and VAT, low-stock alerts.
- **Admin UX:**
  - Mobile card rows for tables.
  - Status tabs with counts on the orders list.
  - A sticky save/publish bar on the product form.
  - A rich-text description editor.
  - A shared KPI card.
  - A bar chart for revenue.
- **Code:**
  - Move the inline `validate()` and inline prop arrays in admin controllers into FormRequests and Resources. Settings, Catalog, Company and Dashboard are the worst offenders.
  - Re-authenticate before staff change their own email or password.
  - Limit cash-on-delivery orders per phone number to stop stock draining.
